Who it's for

One inbox. Two kinds of busy.

Whether recruiters are chasing you or clients are, the hard part is the same: answering everyone well without spending your day in DMs.

For job seekers

Say yes to the right roles, gracefully no to the rest.

Set your rules once. Remote only, where you can work, your pay floor. Every recruiter gets the right reply.

  • Interested and asking smart questions on remote roles
  • A polite "remote only" for hybrid and onsite, no wasted back-and-forth
  • Flags remote roles limited to regions you can't work from
  • Mentions your pay floor only when an offer falls below it
New · For business owners & coaches

Turn inbound DMs into booked calls.

Leads, coaching inquiries, partnership offers and pitches, each answered in your voice with the next step built in.

  • Warm replies to leads that move them toward a call
  • Answers pricing and availability questions from your own details
  • Shares your booking link or portfolio at the right moment
  • Declines vendor pitches kindly, in one line

How it works

Three clicks. Zero copy-paste.

01

Click ✦ Draft

It sits right in every LinkedIn chat, in pop-ups and on the Messaging page. Or press Alt+R.

02

It reads the room

The whole conversation: who said what, the role, location, pay, tech stack, and what they're actually asking.

03

You stay in control

Tweak it with one tap, insert it into the right chat, and press Send yourself.

Features

Small tool. Sharp instincts.

Knows what kind of message it is

Remote role, hybrid, lead, coaching inquiry, pitch, follow-up. Each gets the reply it deserves.

Your templates, your rules

Edit what it says for every category, or add your own. Placeholders fill in names and details.

Spots the tech stack

Lists the technologies they mention, ticks the ones you know, and never asks about what they already told you.

Override anything, per reply

"Call me a Backend Engineer." "Mention I'm free Thursday." One line, and it rewrites around it.

Fast enough to feel instant

Runs on Groq's gpt-oss-120b, so a draft lands in about a second and a half.

Never sends on its own

It only fills in the message box. Every word goes out because you pressed Send.

Install

Up and running in two minutes.

  1. Download and unzipGrab the latest release and unzip it into a folder you'll keep.
  2. Load it in ChromeOpen chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Add your free Groq keyCreate one at console.groq.com/keys and paste it into Settings, which opens by itself.
  4. Tell it about youYour role, skills, rules and links. Then open any LinkedIn chat and click ✦ Draft.
Privacy

No servers. No accounts. No tracking.

Your key and settings stay in your browser. A conversation is sent to Groq only when you click Draft, and only to write that one reply.

The code is open source, so you can read every line of it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. The extension is free and open source, and Groq's free plan covers about three drafts a minute, plenty for a normal inbox.

Will it send messages for me?

Never. It fills in LinkedIn's message box and stops there. You read it, edit it if you like, and press Send.

Why do I need Developer mode?

It's installed straight from GitHub rather than the Chrome Web Store, and Chrome asks for Developer mode to load extensions that way. It takes ten seconds.

How do I update it?

Download the new release, unzip it over the same folder, and click reload on the extension's card. Your settings carry over.

Does it work in Edge or Brave?

They're built on Chrome and load extensions the same way, so it should, though it's only tested in Chrome.

Is it affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. It's an independent open-source tool that helps you write replies on linkedin.com.
