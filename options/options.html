LinkedIn Reply Assistant

Drafts replies to your LinkedIn messages. It only ever fills in the message box; you review and press Send.

How to draft

  1. Click ✨ Draft in any LinkedIn chat's toolbar. It reads that conversation.
  2. Or press Alt+R or click the toolbar icon to draft for the chat you're in.
  3. Or highlight any text (a message, a profile, a post) and right-click → Draft a reply with AI.

Groq connection

Stored only in this browser. It's sent to Groq, and nowhere else, when you draft. Get one at console.groq.com/keys.

On Groq's free plan each model allows about 8,000 tokens a minute, roughly 3 drafts. When the chosen model is busy, the other one is used automatically.
